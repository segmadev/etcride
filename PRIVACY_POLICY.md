## 1. INTRODUCTION

ETCRide is a service operated by **ETC Horizon Global Ltd**, a company incorporated under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria ("the Company", "we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of everyone who uses our mobile application and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over your data. It applies to both the ETCRide customer application and the ETCRide Driver application (together, "the Apps") and all associated services ("the Services").

Please read this policy carefully. By creating an account or using our Services, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree, you must stop using our Services.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration (Customers)**
- Full name
- Phone number (used as primary identifier)
- Email address
- Profile photo (optional)
- Password (stored in encrypted form; never stored in plain text)

**Account Registration (Drivers — via website or admin onboarding)**
- Full name
- Phone number and email address
- Government-issued ID (type, document number, expiry date, scanned copy)
- Driver's licence number and expiry date
- Vehicle registration and insurance documents
- Vehicle photos
- Bank account details for earnings payouts

**Payment Information**
- Card details processed through Flutterwave or Monnify (we store only the last 4 digits and card type; full card numbers are handled exclusively by the payment processor and never stored on our servers)
- Bank account reference numbers for transfer payments
- Transaction history, amounts, and timestamps

**Support and Communication**
- Messages sent to our support team (text, images)
- Trip feedback, ratings, and reviews
- Dispute reports and supporting information

**Account Deletion Requests**
- Reason for deletion, date of request, and verification information

### 2.2 Location Data

We collect precise GPS location information from your device:

- **Real-time location** — collected continuously during active trips for navigation, matching, and safety
- **Pickup and destination addresses** — entered manually or selected from the map by customers
- **Trip route** — the actual path taken from pickup to destination, stored for fare verification and dispute resolution
- **Background location (drivers only)** — collected while the driver app is running in the background and the driver is marked online; this is essential for dispatching nearby jobs

**Retention:** Location data tied to a specific trip is retained for 90 days after the trip ends, or longer if the trip is subject to an active dispute.

If you do not grant location permission, core features of the app (booking, navigation, driver matching) will not function.

### 2.3 Device and Technical Information

- Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
- App version and build number
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications
- IP address at the time of each request
- Crash logs and diagnostic error reports (used solely for technical debugging; personally identifiable information is excluded where possible)

### 2.4 Biometric Data

If you choose to enable biometric sign-in (fingerprint or face recognition):

- Biometric data is processed entirely on your device by the operating system
- No biometric data is transmitted to or stored on ETCRide servers
- You can disable this feature at any time in Settings

### 2.5 Transaction and Financial Data

- Trip fares, breakdown (base fare, distance, waiting time, surge)
- Payment method used per trip
- Refund requests and outcomes
- Driver earnings and payout records

### 2.6 Usage and Behavioural Data

- Trips booked and completed
- Ratings given and received
- In-app actions and navigation patterns
- Cancellation history
- Job acceptance and rejection rates (drivers)

## 4. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

### 4.1 Between Customers and Drivers (During a Trip)

**Customers see:**
- Driver's first name and profile photo
- Vehicle make, model, colour, and licence plate
- Driver's real-time location
- Driver's aggregate rating

**Drivers see:**
- Customer's first name
- Pickup location and destination address
- Customer's contact number (accessible only through the in-app call feature)
- Trip type and any special instructions

We do not share full personal details (surname, full phone number, or home address) between customers and drivers beyond what is listed above.

### 4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with trusted third parties who process it solely on our behalf:

| Provider | Data Shared | Purpose |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **Flutterwave** | Transaction amount, customer reference, email | Card and bank payment processing |
| **Monnify** | Transaction amount, customer reference | Bank transfer processing |
| **Google Maps Platform** | Pickup/destination coordinates, route polyline | Navigation, routing, geocoding |
| **Termii** | Phone number, OTP message content | SMS OTP for authentication |
| **Firebase (Google)** | FCM token, notification content, crash logs | Push notifications and crash analytics |
| **Email/SMTP provider** | Email address, message content | Transactional emails (receipts, confirmations) |

All third-party providers are contractually required to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

### 4.3 Legal and Law Enforcement Disclosures

We may disclose personal data when required to do so by:
- A court order or legal process served on ETC Horizon Global Ltd
- A request from a Nigerian government agency or law enforcement body with appropriate authority
- Protecting the safety of any person where there is an immediate risk of harm
- Defending ETCRide's legal rights in litigation

We will notify affected users of such disclosures where we are legally permitted to do so.

### 4.4 Business Transfers

If ETC Horizon Global Ltd is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify users by in-app notice or email before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 4.5 Aggregated and Anonymised Data

We may share aggregate, de-identified data (such as popular routes, peak hours, and city-level demand statistics) with partners, advertisers, or for research purposes. This data cannot be used to identify any individual user.

### 4.6 Internal Access

Access to personal data within ETC Horizon Global Ltd is restricted on a need-to-know basis:
- **Customer support staff** — access to trip details and account information relevant to resolving your request
- **Finance team** — access to transaction records for accounting and dispute resolution
- **Platform administrators** — full administrative access for platform management

## 5. DATA SECURITY

### 5.1 Technical Measures
- All data transmitted between the Apps and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Passwords are hashed using a strong, one-way cryptographic algorithm and are never stored in plain text
- Payment card data is processed directly by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors; we do not store raw card numbers
- Database access is restricted by role-based access controls and requires authentication

### 5.2 Organisational Measures
- All staff with access to personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations
- Access rights are reviewed periodically and revoked when no longer needed
- We conduct periodic reviews of our security practices

### 5.3 Data Breach Response
In the event of a confirmed personal data breach, we will:
- Investigate and contain the breach as quickly as possible
- Notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery, where technically feasible
- Report the breach to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) as required by the NDPA 2023
- Take corrective action to prevent recurrence

### 5.4 Your Responsibility
You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential. Do not share your password or OTP codes with anyone, including ETCRide support staff (we will never ask for your password). Report any unauthorised access to your account immediately via in-app support.

## 6. DATA RETENTION

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Active account data (name, email, phone) | Until account deletion | Service delivery |
| Trip records (routes, fares, times) | 1 year after trip | Dispute resolution and analytics |
| Real-time location data | 90 days after trip ends | Dispute resolution |
| Payment and financial records | 5 years | Tax and regulatory compliance (FIRS requirements) |
| Support messages | 1 year | Quality assurance and dispute records |
| Crash logs and diagnostics | 90 days | Technical debugging |

**After account deletion is approved:**
- Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, profile photo) are permanently deleted within 30 days
- Financial and transaction records are retained for 5 years as required by Nigerian tax law
- Anonymised trip data (no personal identifiers) may be retained indefinitely for analytics

Data in backup systems may persist for up to 90 additional days before being overwritten.

## 7. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER NIGERIAN DATA PROTECTION LAW

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the NDPR, you have the following rights:

### 7.1 Right of Access
You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days.

### 7.2 Right to Rectification
You may correct inaccurate information directly in app Settings. For data you cannot edit yourself, contact support.

### 7.3 Right to Erasure (Account Deletion)
You may request permanent deletion of your account. See Settings → Delete Account. Certain data (financial records) must be retained by law regardless of deletion.

### 7.4 Right to Restrict Processing
You may ask us to stop using your data in specific ways (for example, opting out of marketing communications).

### 7.5 Right to Data Portability
You may request your personal data in a commonly used, machine-readable format. Contact support to make this request.

### 7.6 Right to Withdraw Consent
Where processing is based on your consent (e.g. marketing), you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 7.7 Right to Lodge a Complaint
If you believe we have mishandled your personal data, you may file a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** at www.ndpc.gov.ng.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **support@etcride.com** or through the Help section in the app.

## APPENDIX: COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: Is my location tracked when I'm not in a trip?**
A: For customers, location is collected only while you have the app open or an active booking. For drivers, location is collected while you are marked as "Online" to enable job dispatching. You can go offline at any time to stop location collection.

**Q: Can I opt out of all data collection?**
A: Some data is essential for the service to function (location for trips, phone number for authentication, payment data for fare processing). Optional data (marketing emails, crash analytics) can be disabled in Settings.

**Q: Is my biometric data on ETCRide's servers?**
A: No. Biometric data (fingerprint, face) is processed only by your device's operating system and is never transmitted to our servers.

**Q: What happens to my data if I delete my account?**
A: Personal identifiers (name, phone, email, photo) are permanently deleted within 30 days. Financial transaction records are retained for 5 years as required by Nigerian law, in anonymised form where possible.

**Q: How do I report a data concern?**
A: Email support@etcride.com with the subject "Privacy Request" or use Help → Contact Support in the app. You can also file a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at www.ndpc.gov.ng.
