## 7. PAYMENTS AND FARES

### 7.1 Fare Calculation
Fares are calculated on the basis of:
- Distance travelled (measured using GPS)
- Trip duration (including any billable waiting time beyond the free waiting period)
- Service type and vehicle category selected
- Surge pricing where demand exceeds supply (shown to the Customer before booking confirmation)
- Any applicable minimum fare or surcharge configured by ETCRide administration

An estimated fare is shown to the Customer before they confirm a Booking. The final fare may differ from the estimate where the actual route or duration differs from the estimate.

### 7.2 Accepted Payment Methods
- Card payments (Visa, Mastercard) via Flutterwave or Monnify
- Bank transfer via Monnify or Flutterwave
- Cash (subject to Driver acceptance — some Drivers may not accept cash)

### 7.3 Payment Timing
Payment may be charged at booking confirmation or upon trip completion, depending on the payment method and the configuration set by ETCRide administration at the time of booking. The applicable payment timing will be displayed clearly before you confirm your Booking.

### 7.4 Failed Payments
If a payment fails after trip completion, you remain liable for the full fare. ETCRide may restrict your account until the outstanding amount is settled.

### 7.5 Driver Earnings
Drivers receive their share of the fare net of the ETCRide service commission, as communicated during driver onboarding. Payout timelines and methods are as communicated by ETCRide administration and may be updated from time to time.

### 7.6 Disputed Charges
You may dispute a charge within **7 days** of the trip. ETCRide will review the dispute using GPS records, trip data, and Driver and Customer accounts. ETCRide's determination will be communicated within a reasonable time and, where a dispute is upheld, a refund will be issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

### 7.7 Refunds
Refunds will not be issued for completed trips unless the dispute process produces a finding in the Customer's favour or ETCRide determines that the fare was incorrectly calculated. Refunds are not available for services that were provided as requested.

### 7.8 Promotions and Discounts
Promotional codes and discounts are issued at ETCRide's discretion. They are non-transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and are subject to the specific terms communicated at the time of issue.
